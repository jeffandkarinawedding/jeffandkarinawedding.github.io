FAQ

The finer details.

Can I bring a plus one? +

Our venue requires numbers to be known in advance, so we can only accommodate the guests named on the email invitations sent out. If you're unsure, please ask us!

Are children invited? +

Similar to the plus one policy, we can only accommodate the guests named on the email invitations sent out. If you're unsure, please ask us!

What time should we arrive? +

Arrival times will depend on which part of the celebration you are joining and may be subject to change. A final schedule will be shared closer to the wedding day.


If attending the ceremony:

Please aim to arrive by 1:00 pm to allow time to be seated before the ceremony begins at 1:30 pm.

Please note: capacity at the ceremony is limited due to Chapel size.


If attending the wedding breakfast:

Please plan to arrive between 2:00 pm and 3:30 pm for drinks and to get seated for the meal beginning at 4:00 pm.

Is there parking at the venue? +

Durham Castle has limited parking available for guests staying overnight.

For day guests, we recommend using public transport or nearby parking facilities in Durham city centre:

  • A full listing of all Durham car parks
  • Prince Bishops Car Park, City, Durham DH1 3UJ - the closest car park, but limited spaces.
  • The Sands Multi-storey, Riverside Place, Durhan, DH1 1SL - a slightly longer walk, but larger capacity.
What if I have undisclosed dietry restrictions, or I become a vegetarian in August? +

Please note any dietary requirements when you RSVP, and we'll make sure you're well looked after. If these dietary restrictions change, please let us know and we can try to accomodate!
